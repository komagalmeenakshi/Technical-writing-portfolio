# How to Change Your Profile Picture

## Before You Begin
- Log in to your account.
- Have your photo saved on your device as a `.jpg` or `.png` file.

## Steps
1. Go to Account Settings.
2. Click **Change Profile Picture**.
3. Upload your photo.
4. Click **Save** to save the changes.

> **Tip:** Use an image smaller than 20 MB.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem             | Cause                             | Fix                              |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| File size exceeded   | File size exceeds 20 MB           | Upload an image smaller than 20 MB |
| No internet          | Internet connection interrupted   | Restart the router               |
